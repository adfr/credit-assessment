# BOARD RESOLUTION
## ACME Corporation

---

## Authorization to Obtain Credit Facility

**Resolution Date:** January 10, 2025
**Meeting Type:** Special Meeting of the Board of Directors
**Location:** Corporate Headquarters, Chicago, IL

---

### Attendees

**Board Members Present:**
- Robert Chen, Chairman and CEO
- David Chen, COO
- Jennifer Martinez, CFO
- Margaret Thompson, Independent Director
- William Foster, Independent Director

**Board Members Absent:**
- None

**Quorum:** A quorum of Directors was present throughout the meeting.

---

### Resolutions

**WHEREAS,** ACME Corporation (the "Company") desires to obtain a credit facility for the purpose of equipment acquisition and facility expansion;

**WHEREAS,** the Company has identified an opportunity to expand its manufacturing capacity through the acquisition of new CNC machining equipment and expansion of its Chicago facility;

**WHEREAS,** the Board of Directors has reviewed and discussed the proposed credit facility and believes it is in the best interest of the Company and its shareholders;

**NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED,** that the Company is hereby authorized to apply for and obtain a credit facility in an amount not to exceed Fifteen Million Dollars ($15,000,000) from one or more financial institutions, on such terms and conditions as the Chief Executive Officer and Chief Financial Officer deem appropriate;

**RESOLVED FURTHER,** that Robert Chen, as Chief Executive Officer, and Jennifer Martinez, as Chief Financial Officer, are hereby authorized, jointly or severally, to:

1. Negotiate and execute loan agreements, promissory notes, security agreements, and all other documents necessary to obtain the credit facility;

2. Pledge the assets of the Company, including but not limited to real property, equipment, inventory, and accounts receivable, as collateral for the credit facility;

3. Execute personal guarantees on behalf of the Company if required by the lender;

4. Take any and all other actions necessary or desirable to effectuate the purposes of these resolutions;

**RESOLVED FURTHER,** that any and all actions heretofore taken by any officer or director of the Company in connection with the matters contemplated by these resolutions are hereby ratified, confirmed, and approved in all respects;

**RESOLVED FURTHER,** that the Secretary of the Company is directed to prepare and maintain a copy of these resolutions in the Company's minute book.

---
